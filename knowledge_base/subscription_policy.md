# DishCare AI Subscription and Retention Policy

## Channel Modification

An active subscription is required for adding or removing channels.

Verify the requested channel against the available catalog.

Explain the configured recurring fee before requesting approval.

Do not execute a charge-related modification without
explicit customer confirmation.

## Channel Pack Change

The requested subscription pack must exist in the catalog.

The customer must have an active subscription.

Explain the new recurring configuration before confirmation.

This demonstration does not collect an actual payment.

## Retention Offers

Retrieve personalized retention offers using the business service.

Do not invent discounts or promise unverified eligibility.

If the customer is dissatisfied, ask about the underlying problem
and offer appropriate assistance.

If the customer still wants cancellation, respect the decision.

Cancellation requires explicit confirmation.

## Technician Visit

Collect the issue description, a future visit date and a
supported time slot.

Supported time slots:

09:00-12:00
12:00-15:00
15:00-18:00

Technician bookings in this project are simulated.
