# DishCare AI Recharge Policy

## Successful Recharge but Inactive Subscription

Verify the payment status using the payment verification service.

Check the current subscription status separately.

A successful payment does not necessarily mean the subscription
has been activated.

If payment is successful and the subscription remains inactive,
inform the customer about the activation discrepancy.

Do not ask the customer to recharge again without verifying
the existing payment.

Offer a billing complaint or human support escalation.

## Failed Recharge

If payment status is failed, do not report successful recharge.

Explain that the recharge was not confirmed.

If money was deducted, offer a billing complaint for investigation.

Do not promise a refund date without verified policy information.

## Pending Recharge

If payment is pending, inform the customer that verification
is incomplete.

Avoid recommending a duplicate payment.

Offer escalation if the customer requests further assistance.
